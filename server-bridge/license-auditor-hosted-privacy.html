Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · ServerBridge License Auditor — Hosted Service

Hosted service documents: Data Processing Agreement · Terms of Service · For the downloadable License Auditor, see its Privacy Policy and Terms.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Lee Crowe Software Solutions LLC (“we”, “us”) provides the ServerBridge License Auditor Hosted Service (“the Service”): a subscription service that audits Microsoft 365 licence usage across the Microsoft 365 tenants a customer connects, and produces a consolidated report.

This policy covers the Service only. The downloadable ServerBridge License Auditor and the free ServerBridge LicenseScan PowerShell module are covered by a separate policy, and neither sends us any tenant data.

2. Two different roles, and why the distinction matters

For your own account information we are the controller. Your name, your contact email, your subscription record — we decide how those are handled, and this policy governs them.

For the directory data we read from the tenants you connect we are a processor, and you are the controller. That data belongs to you and your clients. We read it only to produce your reports, only on your instructions, and we do not use it for any purpose of our own. The terms on which we do this are set out in our Data Processing Agreement, which forms part of your contract.

If the tenants you connect belong to your clients rather than to you, you are responsible for having the authority to connect them and for telling those clients that their data is processed by us.

3. What the Service reads from a connected tenant

Each scan reads, using read-only Microsoft Graph permissions:

  • Licensed user accounts: display name, user principal name, account enabled state, account type (member or guest), and assigned licences.
  • Sign-in activity dates, or, where that is unavailable, Microsoft 365 usage-report activity dates.
  • Subscribed licence inventory for the organisation: product, seats enabled, seats assigned.
  • For each licensed mailbox: mailbox type (user, shared, room, equipment), size, and whether an archive exists.
  • For accounts that look like leavers: the number of groups and directory roles they still belong to. A count is all that is read.

The Service reads nothing else. It never reads message content, file content, calendar content, chat content, or any password or secret. The permissions it holds are read-only and cannot write, modify or delete anything in a connected tenant.

4. What we keep, and what we do not

We keep:

  • The reports we produce for you. A report lists the accounts it identifies as dormant, including their display names and user principal names. Reports therefore contain personal data about individuals in the tenants you connect. That is the purpose of the report.
  • Per-run totals: number of tenants scanned and failed, recoverable annual spend, dormant seat count, unassigned seat count, accounts-to-review count. Numbers only.
  • The list of tenants you have connected: the Microsoft tenant ID and the organisation’s name.
  • Your own account information: your name, contact email, and the subscription record Microsoft sends us. We never see or hold your payment details.

We do not keep:

  • Any credential belonging to you or to a connected tenant. None is ever created, transmitted or stored. Access is granted when a tenant’s administrator consents to our application, and we then authenticate as ourselves using a certificate we hold. There is no customer password, secret or token anywhere in the Service.
  • A database of the users in your tenants. The per-user rows a scan reads exist only in memory for the duration of that scan and are discarded when the report has been produced. What survives is the report and the totals above.
  • Message, file, calendar or chat content, which the Service never reads at all.

5. Your own account information

  • Billing. The Service is sold only through the Microsoft commercial marketplace. Microsoft bills you; we never see or hold your payment details at all, and no payment processor of ours is involved. We receive the subscription identifier and the information Microsoft provides with it.
  • Email. We send your name and contact email to Brevo so we can deliver your reports, service notices and account email. You may unsubscribe from non-essential email at any time.
  • Correspondence. If you contact us for support, we keep that correspondence to answer you.

6. Where the Service runs, and who else is involved

The Service runs on Microsoft Azure in the Azure Central US region, in the United States. Reports are stored in Azure Blob Storage with no public access; they are retrievable only through a short-lived, single-purpose link issued to a signed-in user of your account.

Our sub-processors:

Sub-processor Purpose Data involved
Microsoft Azure Hosting, storage, key management Everything the Service holds
Microsoft (Graph) Source of the data audited Read-only access to connected tenants
Microsoft commercial marketplace Billing and subscription management Your subscription record
Brevo Transactional email Your name and contact email

We will give notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor, as set out in the DPA.

7. How long we keep things

  • Reports: 13 months. Long enough to compare a month with the same month last year; the per-run totals below cover longer comparisons, so older reports are deleted to limit what a breach could expose. You may delete any report at any time from the portal.
  • Per-run totals: for the life of your subscription, so month-over-month comparison works.
  • Connected tenant records: for the life of your subscription, including tenants whose consent has been withdrawn, so you can see what happened to them.
  • Account and subscription records: for the life of your subscription, plus the period required for tax and accounting purposes (typically up to seven years for financial records).

On termination we delete your reports, totals and tenant records within thirty (30) days, except where law requires retention.

8. Ending access

A connected tenant’s administrator can withdraw our access at any time from their own Microsoft 365 admin centre, without going through us, and without needing our cooperation. When access is withdrawn the tenant stops being scanned. We keep the record of that tenant, marked as revoked, so it does not silently disappear from your reports — if you want it removed entirely, you can delete it.

Where you are a data subject in relation to the account information we hold as controller, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability, or object to processing. Email hello@leecrowesoftware.com. We respond to verified requests within forty-five (45) days, extendable once by a further forty-five (45) days with notice and reasons.

Where the request concerns directory data we process on your behalf — for example, a request from an employee of one of your clients — the request belongs to you as controller. We will tell you promptly, will not respond directly unless you instruct us to, and will help you respond.

9.1 Kentucky residents (KCDPA)

Under the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act, Kentucky residents have the right to: confirm whether we process their personal data; access it; correct inaccuracies; delete it; obtain a portable copy; and opt out of processing for targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or profiling with significant legal effects. We do none of those three things, but the right exists and is stated here.

9.2 How to exercise them, and how to appeal

Email hello@leecrowesoftware.com. We respond to verified requests within forty-five (45) days. If we decline to act, you may appeal within sixty (60) days by replying to that address; we will tell you the outcome and our reasons. If the appeal is denied you may contact the Kentucky Attorney General using the Attorney General's online consumer data privacy complaint form.

10. Where data is held, and customers outside the United States

The Service is offered to customers in the United States. All processing and storage takes place in the United States, and we do not currently offer the Service to customers established in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom.

If you connect a tenant whose users are located outside the United States, their personal data will be processed in the United States. As controller, it is your responsibility to ensure you have a lawful basis and, where required, a valid transfer mechanism for doing so.

11. Security, and telling you when something goes wrong

We protect the Service with: encryption in transit and at rest; no customer credentials to steal; private report storage with short-lived access links; a single service certificate held in Azure Key Vault; and least-privilege, read-only access to connected tenants.

If a breach affects personal data we process on your behalf, we will notify you without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it, with the information you need to meet your own notification obligations. If a breach affects your account information, we will notify you directly.

Kentucky residents. Where unencrypted personal information (as defined in KRS 365.732 and the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act) was, or is reasonably believed to have been, acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify the affected Kentucky resident in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, consistent with the legitimate needs of law enforcement and any measures necessary to determine the scope of the breach and restore the integrity of the system.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we do not claim otherwise.
