Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · ServerBridge License Auditor — Hosted Service

Hosted service documents: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · For the downloadable License Auditor, see its Privacy Policy and Terms.

This Agreement governs our processing of personal data on your behalf when you use the ServerBridge License Auditor Hosted Service. It forms part of, and is subject to, the Terms of Service between us. Where this Agreement conflicts with the Terms on the subject of data protection, this Agreement prevails.

Parties. “Processor”, “we”, “us” means Lee Crowe Software Solutions LLC. “Controller”, “you” means the customer identified in the subscription.

1. Roles

You are the controller of the personal data contained in the Microsoft 365 tenants you connect. We are a processor acting on your documented instructions. Where you connect a tenant belonging to a client of yours, you warrant that you are authorised to do so and that you have met any obligation to inform that client.

2. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose

Subject matter: auditing Microsoft 365 licence assignment and account activity. Duration: the term of your subscription, plus the deletion period in clause 9. Nature and purpose: reading licence and activity data from connected tenants in order to identify unused and underused licences, and producing reports for you.

3. Categories of data subject and personal data

Data subjects: licensed user accounts in the tenants you connect — typically employees, contractors and guests of your organisation or your clients’.

Personal data processed:

  • Display name and user principal name (which is usually an email address)
  • Account enabled state and account type (member or guest)
  • Assigned licences
  • Last sign-in or last activity date
  • Mailbox type, mailbox size, and whether an archive exists

No special category data is processed. We do not read message, file, calendar or chat content, and the access we hold cannot write to or alter a connected tenant.

4. Your instructions

We process personal data only on your documented instructions, which comprise this Agreement, the Terms, and your use of the Service’s own functions (connecting a tenant, running a scan, downloading or deleting a report). We will tell you if we believe an instruction infringes applicable data protection law.

We will not use the personal data we process on your behalf for any purpose of our own, and in particular will not use it to train machine-learning models, to build any product, or for marketing.

For the avoidance of doubt, this does not restrict our use of our own operational telemetry — run durations, error rates, counts of tenants scanned — which contains no personal data from your tenants and is how the Service is kept working.

5. Confidentiality

Access to personal data processed under this Agreement is limited to personnel who need it to operate the Service, who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

  • No customer credentials. Access to a connected tenant is granted by that tenant’s own administrator consenting to our application. We authenticate using a certificate held in Azure Key Vault. We create, transmit and store no password, secret or token belonging to you or to a connected tenant.
  • Read-only access. The permissions granted are read-only and cannot modify a connected tenant.
  • Minimised retention. The per-user data read during a scan exists only in memory for the duration of that scan and is discarded once the report is produced. What is retained is the report itself, aggregate totals, and the list of connected tenants.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Private report storage, accessible only through short-lived links issued to signed-in users of your account.

7. Sub-processors

You authorise the sub-processors listed in our Privacy Policy. We impose data protection obligations on each that are no less protective than those in this Agreement, and we remain responsible to you for their performance.

We will give you thirty (30) days’ notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor. If you reasonably object on data protection grounds within that period, you may terminate the affected subscription without penalty. Because Microsoft bills and refunds marketplace purchases, we will cancel the subscription on your request and support your request to Microsoft for a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees under its marketplace refund procedures.

8. Assistance

Data subject requests. If we receive a request from a data subject relating to personal data we process on your behalf, we will not respond to it substantively, will tell you without undue delay, and will help you respond, taking into account the nature of the processing.

Personal data breach. We will notify you without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting personal data processed on your behalf. The notification will describe what we know of the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

Impact assessments. We will provide reasonable assistance with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations, taking into account the information available to us.

9. Deletion and return

On termination you may export your reports for thirty (30) days. At the end of that period we delete the reports, aggregate totals and connected tenant records we hold for you, unless law requires retention — in which case we will tell you what is retained and why, and continue to protect it under this Agreement. Deleted reports remain in a protected recovery copy for up to fourteen (14) days, solely to recover from accidental deletion, and are then permanently erased.

Nothing we hold includes a copy of a connected tenant’s directory, so no such copy needs returning.

10. Audit

We will make available the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement. On at least thirty (30) days’ prior written notice, and no more than once per calendar year, we will allow for and contribute to reasonable inspections by you, or by an independent assessor you designate who has first entered into a confidentiality agreement with us, to evaluate compliance with this Agreement and with the KCDPA. Inspections are at your expense and take place during normal business hours in a way that does not disrupt the Service. We may satisfy that requirement by providing a report of an assessment conducted by an independent assessor using an appropriate and accepted control framework, the results of which will be provided to you on request.

11. Location of processing

All processing under this Agreement takes place in the United States, in the Azure Central US region. We will not move processing outside the United States without giving you notice and putting an appropriate transfer mechanism in place.

12. Liability

Liability under this Agreement is subject to the limitations and exclusions in the Terms of Service.
