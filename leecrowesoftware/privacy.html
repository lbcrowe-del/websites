Privacy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page covers this website, leecrowesoftware.com, run by Lee Crowe Software Solutions LLC. Each product has its own privacy policy: ServerBridge, License Auditor and Halera.

What this website records

We use PostHog to see how people find this site. For each visit it records the page address (including any campaign tags in it, such as utm_source), the referring site, your browser type and approximate location.

It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It doesn't build a profile of you, and we don't use it to identify you. Because nothing is stored on your device, there's no consent banner. PostHog, in the United States, processes this data for us.

When you use the contact form

The form sends us what you type into it: your name, email address, company (if you give one), the topic you pick and your message. We use it to reply to you and, if we go on to work together, to plan that work. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Your message is delivered to our inbox by Brevo, our email provider, based in France. It is then kept in our Microsoft 365 mailbox as ordinary business correspondence. Ask us to delete it at any time.

To stop the form being flooded, our server briefly counts how many messages arrive from each IP address. The address is scrambled before it is counted, is held only in memory, and is never written down.
