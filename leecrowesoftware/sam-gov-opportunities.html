How it works

About ten minutes of setup, once. It runs on Apify, so there's nothing to install.

  1. Get a free SAM.gov key. Sign in to SAM.gov and request a Public API Key under Account Details. It's stored as a secret and never appears in your results.
  2. Set up your search. Open the finder on Apify, enter your NAICS codes and other filters, turn on "Only new or changed notices", and save it as a task.
  3. Schedule it. Add an Apify schedule, for example 7:00 every weekday.
  4. Choose where it goes. Connect Gmail, Slack or Google Sheets, and the new notices arrive there after each run.

Questions

Why do I need my own SAM.gov key?

SAM.gov's terms don't allow sharing a personal API key, so each user brings their own. The key is free. Your key's daily request limit applies, and the finder stops cleanly at that limit and keeps everything it already collected.

Does it scrape SAM.gov?

No. It uses the official SAM.gov Get Opportunities Public API and the public USAspending.gov API. It doesn't log in anywhere for you.

Do the links need a SAM.gov login?

No. Every notice has a public sam.gov link that opens in any browser.

Are attachments downloaded?

No. You get the links SAM.gov publishes for each notice's attachments.

Do I need an Apify account?

Yes. The finder runs on Apify, and charges appear on your Apify bill. Signing up is free.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes. If you'd rather have the searches, schedule and delivery set up for your team, or need it connected to your own systems, get in touch.
